Our Beginning

Little Haven was born from a simple belief: the space where you welcome your child should be as beautiful, calming, and safe as the rest of your home. We wanted to create nursery furniture that parents didn't have to compromise on—pieces that blend seamlessly with premium interiors while upholding the highest safety standards.

Our Design Philosophy

We believe in timeless design over passing trends. Our furniture is crafted with clean lines, soft natural tones, and adaptable features, ensuring that it remains beautiful long after the nursery years are over.

Meet The Founders

Emma & David

When we were expecting our first child, we struggled to find nursery furniture that met our safety standards without compromising on design. Everything felt either too mass-produced or prohibitively expensive.

With David's background in woodworking and Emma's eye for interior design, we decided to build our own crib. That single piece of furniture sparked the idea for Little Haven. Today, we're proud to bring our thoughtfully designed, sustainably crafted pieces into your home.
